Thomas Ferro - Software developer

As a full-stack developer, I am driven by a passion for my craft and a desire to apply my expertise for the benefit of clients and users.

I am motivated by designing high-quality solutions, particularly through methodologies that place business needs at the heart of development (Software Craftsmanship, Domain-Driven Design, Agile methodologies). My approach to software quality rests on two key pillars.

First: interpersonal quality. This involves active listening, clarifying misunderstandings, discussing potential solutions, and expressing needs; all while working within diverse team environments.

Second: technical quality. This means using the right tool for the job, finding the most relevant solution for a specific context, and demonstrating system reliability, partly through well-tailored testing suites.
